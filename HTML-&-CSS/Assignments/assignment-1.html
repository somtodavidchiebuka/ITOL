Jujutsu Kaisen is a dark fantasy manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump from March 2018 to September 2024. The story follows high school student Yuji Itadori, who becomes the host of Ryomen Sukuna, a powerful ancient curse, after swallowing one of Sukuna’s fingers to protect others. Yuji joins the Tokyo Jujutsu High School, a secret organization of sorcerers who use Cursed Energy to combat malevolent spirits known as Curses.
